What is RabbiHossainLTD?

RabbiHossainLTD is an independent software and digital services brand founded in 2024 and operated by MD Rabbi Hossain from Kaliganj, Jhenaidah, Khulna, Bangladesh.

The brand focuses first on web, app and custom software development, API integration, automation, UI/UX, branding and website security. Selected digital store services—including game top-ups, premium apps and card services—are also offered through the same platform.

Legal clarification: RabbiHossainLTD is currently a personal brand and online business, not a registered limited company. “LTD” is part of the brand name and does not claim legal incorporation.
